Starlanes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

This policy explains how the Starlanes mobile game ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. Starlanes is published by Vidnovatech. If you have questions, contact us at vidnovatech@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Starlanes has no accounts, logins or sign-ups.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information ourselves.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress (unlocked levels, stars, hints, settings such as sound and vibration, and the pictures you have collected) in local storage on your device. This data is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Starlanes is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data related to ads

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. Google uses it to deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show personalised ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where required by law, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. To change your choice later, clear the app's data (or reinstall it) and reopen it to be asked again.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Your data: because we do not hold any personal data about you, we have nothing to give you, correct or delete. To delete the progress saved on your device, uninstall the app or clear its storage. For data held by Google, use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com.

Children

Starlanes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone other than the advertising service described above.

Security

Communication between the app and Google's ad services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled, such as analytics or in-app purchases, we will update this page and its effective date before those features are released.
